# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses. This glossary fixes the words we use for the business, in the product, in the code and in conversation.

## People

**Buyer**:
The person who paid for a purchase, whether for their own seat or for team seats.
_Avoid_: Customer, learner, user (when you mean who paid)

**Student**:
A person taking a course. A Student may or may not be the Buyer.
_Avoid_: Learner

**Admin**:
A Cadence staff member. Only an Admin approves or declines a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Staff, team (when you mean us)

## Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A Buyer's ask to get their money back for one purchase, which an Admin then approves or declines. A purchase has at most one Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, chargeback, reversal, refund (when you mean the ask, not the money)

**Refund**:
The money we return when an Admin approves a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Reversal, reimbursement

**Refund Reason**:
The Buyer's own words on why they want their money back, given with every Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Reason (alone), note, complaint

**Decline Reason**:
The Admin's explanation, required with every decline, that the Buyer receives.
_Avoid_: Reason (alone), rejection note

**Refund Amount**:
What the Buyer paid for the purchase, fixed when they make the Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Refundable amount, list price

**Requested**:
The state of a Refund Request that no Admin has decided yet. The Buyer keeps full access meanwhile.
_Avoid_: Pending, open, raised

**Approved**:
The state of a Refund Request an Admin accepted, meaning the Admin has returned the Refund Amount. The purchase no longer gives access to the course; the Student's progress stays on record.
_Avoid_: Cancelled, reversed, completed

**Declined**:
The state of a Refund Request an Admin turned down: the Buyer keeps the course and gets no money back. The decision is final.
_Avoid_: Rejected, denied

**Refund Policy**:
The rules a purchase must meet, checked once when the Buyer makes a Refund Request: inside the Refund Window, under 2 hours watched, under half the lessons completed, and, for team seats, no seat claimed yet. A Refund always covers the whole purchase.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer, eligibility, money-back guarantee

**Refund Window**:
The 14 calendar days after a purchase, counting the whole of the last day, in which the Buyer may make a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Cancel window

**Chargeback**:
A Buyer's dispute raised through their bank or card issuer, not through us. Never use it for a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Dispute (except when quoting the payment provider)
